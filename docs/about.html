About

Scripture for ordinary moments.

The Vivle is a quiet Christian Bible companion made for reading and reflection throughout the day.

The app

What you can do

Read an hourly verse, explore another passage with a swipe, search by exact reference or keyword, and save verses you want to revisit.

Bible text

The app contains all 66 books and 31,102 verses of the standard 1769 King James Version. The source dataset is public domain under the Unlicense.

Christian, not denominational

The Vivle is intended for Christians across traditions. It is independently made and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any church or denomination.

Design approach

The interface is deliberately restrained. There are no social feeds or advertisements competing with the biblical text.
