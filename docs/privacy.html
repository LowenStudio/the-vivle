Privacy policy

Your reading stays yours.

The Vivle is designed to work without an account and without collecting your reading activity.

Policy

Effective date: 27 July 2026

Information we collect

The Vivle does not require registration and does not collect names, email addresses, Bible searches, saved verses, or reading history.

Information stored on your device

Your name preference, saved verses, interface language, appearance setting, onboarding status, and widget values are stored locally using Apple platform storage. Removing the app may remove this local data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple’s notification system stores your permission choice. The app may schedule a local morning verse notification. The notification content is generated on your device.

Third parties and advertising

The Vivle contains no advertising SDK and does not sell or share personal data. Links opened from Settings are handled by your browser and are subject to the browser’s and hosting provider’s own practices.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before or when the relevant app update is released.
