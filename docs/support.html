Before writing

A verse does not appear

Confirm that the app is fully installed, then close and reopen it. The complete Bible is bundled with the app and does not require an internet connection.

Notifications do not arrive

Open iOS Settings, find The Vivle, and confirm that notifications are allowed. Focus modes and notification summaries may delay delivery.

Include in a report

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version
  • The app version shown at the bottom of Settings
  • What you expected and what happened
  • Steps that make the issue happen again
